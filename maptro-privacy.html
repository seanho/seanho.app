Maptro Privacy Policy

Apps by Sean Ho

Last updated 7 November 2026

Maptro shows Melbourne's metro trains, their departures and service disruptions. It doesn't collect, store or share your personal information. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

Your location

If you allow it, Maptro uses your location on your device to show you on the map, to find the stations near you, and to open each line in the direction you're likely to travel. Your location never leaves your device: Maptro doesn't send it to us or to anyone else. You can turn off location access at any time in Settings, and the app still works without it.

Train information

To show timetables, train positions and disruptions, Maptro requests them from Public Transport Victoria's public Timetable API. These requests ask only for train information, such as a line or a station, and contain nothing that identifies you. As with any internet request, Public Transport Victoria's servers can see your device's IP address.

The maps are provided by Apple Maps, under Apple's Privacy Policy.

Translations

When the app is in Chinese or Japanese, disruptions are translated by Apple's translation on your device. Your device may download Apple's language files to do this.

What's kept on your device

Maptro keeps your starred lines, the direction you choose for each line at each time of day, and your widgets' stations on your device only. Deleting the app removes them.

Children

Maptro doesn't collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

For questions about this policy, email maptro@seanho.app.
