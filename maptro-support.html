Maptro Support

Apps by Sean Ho

Maptro shows every Melbourne metro train live on a map, each line's next trains at every station, and the next trains from your station in widgets on your Home Screen, Lock Screen and Apple Watch.

For help, or to report a problem, email maptro@seanho.app. It helps to say which line or station you were looking at, and when.

Adding a widget

Touch and hold an empty part of your Home Screen, tap Edit, then Add Widget, and search for Maptro. Next Trains shows one direction from a station, and Station shows both directions side by side. To choose the station, direction or line, touch and hold the widget and tap Edit Widget.

For your Lock Screen, touch and hold the Lock Screen, tap Customise, choose the Lock Screen, then tap the area under the clock to add Next Trains.

On Apple Watch, add Next Trains to your Smart Stack or to a watch face.

Translated disruptions

When Maptro is in Traditional Chinese, Simplified Chinese or Japanese, disruptions are translated on your iPhone. Maptro follows your iPhone's language, or you can choose its language in Settings, then Apps, then Maptro. The first time, your iPhone may ask to download the language. Tap Show Original to read the English.

Train times and positions

Train times, positions and disruptions come from Public Transport Victoria. A train shows on the map only while it reports its position, so a train may be missing or a little behind where it is. When trains aren't running, such as late at night, the map is empty.

Privacy

Maptro doesn't collect any personal information. Read the Maptro Privacy Policy.
